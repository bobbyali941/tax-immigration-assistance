SMS Messaging Program

Clients may voluntarily enroll in SMS text messaging from Tax & Immigration Assistance SVCS by reviewing and submitting our electronic consent form in person at our office. By selecting the optional SMS consent checkbox and submitting the form, clients agree to receive recurring text messages related to services they have requested.

Types of Messages

Messages may include immigration case updates, appointment reminders, application status notifications, document requests, and other service-related communications.

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency varies depending on your case and service activity. Message and data rates may apply.

How to Opt Out

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, you will no longer receive SMS messages unless you provide consent again.

Consent Is Voluntary

Consent to receive SMS messages is optional and is not a condition of receiving tax preparation, immigration assistance, or any other office service. Clients may decline SMS enrollment and still receive our services.
